Freeze & Thaw Guide
Freezing
After first rise, divide into balls. Coat in oil. Freeze in zip bags up to 3 months.
Thawing
Move to fridge night before. Let come to room temp 1–2 hrs before shaping.
Same-Day
Thaw at room temp 4–6 hrs. Keep covered to prevent drying out.
