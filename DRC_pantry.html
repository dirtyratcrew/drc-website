What's Always in the DRC Kitchen
The Crew's
Pantry.
These are the ingredients the crew keeps stocked at all times. Not obscure, not expensive — just the right things. Know your ingredients and the recipes write themselves.
Crew Staple
🌶️ Gochujang
CJ Haechandle — Crew Pick
Korean fermented red chili paste — sweet, savory, and spicy all at once. Not hot sauce. A fermented paste that adds depth to everything it touches. Get the 500g tub.
Used in: Korean Fried Chicken · Korean Pork Bowl · Galbi Jjim
Walmart ↗
Crew Staple
🍶 Soy Sauce
Kikkoman — Standard
The most-used ingredient in the vault after salt. Naturally brewed, not chemically processed. Get the large bottle. You will go through it.
Used in: Pad Thai · Pork Adobo · Chow Mein · + 20 more
Walmart ↗
Buy This Exact One
🐟 Fish Sauce
Red Boat 40°N
Two ingredients: anchovies and salt. That's it. Red Boat is the only fish sauce worth buying. Everything else has additives. A tablespoon changes a dish completely.
Used in: Pad Thai · Lemongrass Chicken · Vietnamese dishes
Walmart ↗
Crew Staple
🫙 Sesame Oil
Kadoya — Finishing Oil Only
Never cook with this. It burns. This is a finishing oil — a teaspoon at the end of a Korean bowl or stir fry completely changes the dish. Kadoya is the brand Korean households use.
Used in: Korean Pork Bowl · LA Galbi · Chow Mein
Walmart ↗
Always Buy
🥢 Hoisin Sauce
Lee Kum Kee
Fermented soybean paste — sweet, thick, deeply savory. Lee Kum Kee is the standard found in Chinese households worldwide. Don't try to make this at home.
Used in: Moo Shu Chicken · Chow Mein · BBQ Glazes
Walmart ↗
Crew Secret Weapon
🧂 Slap Ya Mama
Cajun Seasoning
Goes in the mac and cheese, on fried chicken, on eggs, on roast potatoes — on almost anything that needs a little something extra. Once it's in your kitchen you won't stop.
Used in: Mac & Cheese · Sinner Chicken · Everything
Walmart ↗
Recommended
🥑 Avocado Oil
Chosen Foods — High Heat
500°F+ smoke point. The oil to use when you need real heat — searing steaks, smash burgers, stir fry. Neutral flavor, better fat profile than vegetable oil. Replaces veg oil in almost every recipe.
Used in: Smash Burger · Stir Fries · Searing
Walmart ↗
Good Find
🥥 Coconut Aminos
Coconut Secret — Soy-Free Swap
Soy-free, gluten-free alternative to soy sauce. Slightly sweeter and less salty. The crew keeps this for guests with soy allergies and for Korean BBQ glazes where the sweetness works.
Used in: Korean BBQ · Marinades · Stir Fries
Walmart ↗
Recommended
🟤 Tamarind Concentrate
Tamicon — Best Value
The sour backbone of Pad Thai. The concentrate jar is easier than a tamarind block and just as good. Store in the fridge after opening.
Used in: Pad Thai · Pad Thai Sauce
