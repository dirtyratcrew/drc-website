These are the ingredients the crew keeps stocked at all times. Know your ingredients and the recipes write themselves.
Crew Staple
Gochujang
CJ Haechandle
Korean fermented red chili paste. Not hot sauce — a fermented paste that adds depth to everything. Get the 500g tub, not the small bottle.
Used in: Korean Fried Chicken · Korean Pork Bowl · Galbi Jjim
Walmart
Crew Staple
Soy Sauce
Kikkoman
The most-used ingredient in the vault after salt. Naturally brewed. Get the large bottle. You will go through it.
Used in: Pad Thai · Pork Adobo · Chow Mein · +20 more
Walmart
Buy This Exact One
Fish Sauce
Red Boat 40N
Two ingredients: anchovies and salt. Red Boat is the only fish sauce worth buying. Everything else has additives.
Used in: Pad Thai · Lemongrass Chicken
Walmart
Crew Staple
Toasted Sesame Oil
Kadoya — Finishing Oil Only
Never cook with this. It burns. Finishing oil only — a teaspoon at the end of a Korean bowl or stir fry changes the dish completely.
Used in: Korean Pork Bowl · LA Galbi · Chow Mein
Walmart
Always Buy
Hoisin Sauce
Lee Kum Kee
Fermented soybean paste — sweet, thick, deeply savory. Don't try to make this at home. Buy it.
Used in: Moo Shu Chicken · Chow Mein · BBQ Glazes
Walmart
Crew Secret Weapon
Slap Ya Mama
Cajun Seasoning
Goes on mac and cheese, fried chicken, eggs, roast potatoes — almost anything that needs something extra. Once it's in your kitchen you won't stop.
Used in: Mac & Cheese · Sinner Chicken · Everything
Walmart
Recommended
Avocado Oil
Chosen Foods — High Heat
500F+ smoke point. Use for searing steaks, smash burgers, stir fry. Neutral flavor, better fat profile than vegetable oil.
Used in: Smash Burger · Stir Fries · Searing
Walmart
Recommended
Tamarind Concentrate
Tamicon
The sour backbone of Pad Thai. Concentrate jar is easier than a block and just as good. Store in fridge after opening.
Used in: Pad Thai

Coconut Secret — Soy-Free Swap
Soy-free, gluten-free alternative to soy sauce. Slightly sweeter. Great for Korean BBQ glazes and guests with soy allergies.
